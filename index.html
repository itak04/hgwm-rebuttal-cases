Reading the metrics如何理解指标

Path reduction is relative to WMNav: 100 × (WMNav path − Full path) / WMNav path. Decision cycles count high-level decisions, not primitive actions or model calls. These route comparisons do not establish lower inference cost.路径减少比例以WMNav为基准：100 × (WMNav路径 − Full路径) / WMNav路径。决策周期是高层决策数，不是底层动作或模型调用次数；路线比较不证明推理成本更低。
